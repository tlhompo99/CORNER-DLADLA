Our Story

The Heart of Mohlakeng

Corner Dladla isn't just a restaurant — it's a kasi institution. Born in the vibrant streets of Mohlakeng, we've become the go-to spot for authentic South African flavour that hits different.

From our famous Mogodu Pap and Salsa to our perfectly grilled Oxliver, every plate is crafted with soul. Whether you're dining in, grabbing takeaway, or chilling on our outdoor seating with the crew — Corner Dladla is where flavour meets vibe.
